# Authors

## Original designer and author

**Rob Cooke** — original designer and author of **CoreDRP (Core Durable Relay Protocol)**, 2026.

CoreDRP was conceived while designing a durable replacement for Miningcore's legacy ZeroMQ share-relay architecture and was subsequently separated into a reusable core protocol, mining profile, and Miningcore integration profile.

Future contributors are recorded permanently through the Git history and project changelog. Attribution of subsequent contributions does not alter the record of CoreDRP's original design and authorship.
