- exact reviewed protobuf blob fingerprints replace the hand-maintained partial-surface parser as the merge gate;
- state vectors use explicit `case_kind` and now cover receiver replacement, wildcard gaps, unlisted senders, clock state updates, event-time bounds, scope ownership and new flow charging;
- supplemental Draft 0.5 vectors cover exact-version profile negotiation, admission digest, ADMIN ordering, scope-digest/ASCII validation and candidate referential integrity;
- Bitcoin validator rejects duplicate txids and executes negative evidence mutations;
- Draft 0.5 specification explicitly states CI/model checks are not a proof of the whole protocol.

## Deliberately retained design boundaries

- CoreDRP remains non-Byzantine: authenticated malicious senders are outside `PayoutSafe` truth guarantees.
- ChainProbe remains diagnostic only; ordinary reconciliation uses the committed head at `C` because the hash chain commits all predecessors.
- Network identity for direct Bitcoin evidence remains owned by the selected Mining scope contract rather than duplicated into Miningcore candidate payloads.
